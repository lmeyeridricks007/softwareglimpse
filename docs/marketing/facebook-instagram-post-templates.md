# Facebook & Instagram post templates

Companion to the [strategy](./facebook-instagram-content-strategy-2026-08.md) and [calendar](./facebook-instagram-calendar-2026-08-to-2027-02.md).

Write **Facebook first** (it carries the URL). Cut for Instagram. Do not invent scores, live prices, or hands-on tests.

---

## 1. Sunday 90-minute batch

1. Open the calendar week. Click every destination. If the page is thin, use the swap board. Note **Promote** and **Spend** — if Spend is not $0, the legal gate must already be open (see calendar).
2. Draft 4 Facebook captions in one note (this file’s formulas).
3. Duplicate → IG: shorter, hook on line 1, hashtags at the end, URL in first comment.
4. Record the Thursday Reel (phone vertical or desktop cropped to 9:16). Hook spoken in the first sentence.
5. Two carousels: 8 slides, 1080×1350, navy/blue/white.
6. Schedule in Meta Business Suite: Tue 09:30, Thu 12:00, Sat 10:00, Sun 11:00 (local). Adjust after four weeks of insights.
7. Put Wed/Fri Story bullets in your phone notes.

## 2. Caption formulas

### Facebook (link in the caption)

```text
{HOOK — specific, slightly sharp, no hype}

{2–4 sentences of teaching. Prefer “if / then” over “best”.}

{What to do next, one verb.}

{FULL URL with UTM}

—
SoftwareGlimpse may earn a commission if you buy through our links. That does not change scores or Finder order.
```

Skip the commission sentence on **tool-only** posts that name no vendor. Keep the last clause if you mention a shortlist of products.

End with a real question when you want comments: “What constraint is actually driving your shortlist — budget, integrations, or admin time?”

### Instagram feed / carousel

```text
{HOOK}

{Same teaching, shorter. Line breaks every 1–2 sentences.}

Save this if you’re {in a demo week / replacing a CRM / doing year-end budget}.

Link in bio → Tools
```

Hashtags (max 8, rotate): `#crmsoftware #salessoftware #b2bsales #revops #saas #founders #softwarebuying`

First comment: full URL (IG) + disclosure if you named products.

### Reel (on-screen + spoken)

| Second | Spoken / on-screen |
| --- | --- |
| 0–1.5 | Hook only. No logo. |
| 1.5–20 | One problem, one mechanism (Finder questions, TCO line items, demo script). |
| 20–35 | Show the actual UI. |
| 35–40 | CTA: “Free, no account — SoftwareGlimpse tools.” |

Caption: hook + 3 lines + “Full tool in bio” + hashtags. Do not bury the hook under a disclaimer; put disclosure after the CTA.

### Reel audio (Meta-safe)

Search **from the Business account** in Reels → Add audio. Business profiles cannot use most chart songs; if a track is missing, it is restricted — do not work around that with a personal login.

| Reel type | What to search | Volume | Avoid |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Founder / methodology (Week 1 Thu) | `lofi chill instrumental` · `calm documentary` · `soft piano ambient` | 12–18% (6–10% with VO) | Pop, memes, phonk |
| Tool demo (Finder, calculator) | `lofi beats` · `ui ambient` · `soft clicks` | 10–15% | Vocals over UI |
| Comparison / “pick this if” | Same lo-fi bed + optional `aha` / `reveal` SFX **once** at the verdict slide | Bed 10%; SFX short | “Oh No”, comedy stings |
| Talking-head | **Your voice is the audio.** Optional bed: `soft piano ambient` | Bed ≤8% | Trending songs under speech |

**Always:** instrumental, no lyrics competing with on-screen type, ≥ the Reel length, start at 0:00.  
**Never for SoftwareGlimpse:** Taylor Swift / Noah Kahan seasonal tracks, Ian McConnell “Bangladesh”, phonk, “It’s Corn”, dance sounds, ad-jingle “success” sax.

Facebook: Meta Sound Collection → Instrumental → Calm / Documentary / Corporate. Re-add on Facebook if IG cross-post drops the track.

## 3. Carousel structures (reuse these)

### Tool explain (6 slides)

1. Hook  
2. Who it’s for  
3. What you enter  
4. What you get  
5. What it will **not** do (no magic ranking, no invented prices)  
6. CTA + path + logo

### Comparison / named product (8 slides)

1. “Not ‘which is best’ — which job.”  
2. Product A: for  
3. Product A: not for  
4. Product B: for  
5. Product B: not for  
6. Shared trap (price, lock-in, overlapping tools)  
7. How we’d decide (point at Finder or scorecard)  
8. CTA + disclosure

### Checklist / resource (7 slides)

1. Hook (“Don’t sit a CRM demo without this”)  
2–5. Items (one per slide, short)  
6. Why a download beats a screenshot  
7. `/resources/{slug}/`

### Methodology (6 slides)

1. The bad pattern: SEO article → affiliate link  
2. Facts vs judgment  
3. Finder does not read commissions  
4. What we still won’t claim  
5. Where to read the full method  
6. CTA

## 5. Disclosure checklist (every post)

- [ ] Named a vendor? Commission sentence in FB caption + IG first comment.  
- [ ] Mentioned a score? It matches the live page and is not a universal #1.  
- [ ] Mentioned price? Qualified as list/example, pointed at calculator or vendor.  
- [ ] Said “we tested”? Only if that product’s assessment has documented hands-on testing.  
- [ ] Destination opened in a browser this week.  
- [ ] Partial tool? The word **partial** or “limited” is in the caption.

### Thu 20 Aug — Why I rebuilt it (E, Reel)

**Spoken hook:** “Choosing software is expensive to reverse.”

I built the original SoftwareGlimpse as a more conventional review site. That model pushes pages, not decisions.

The rebuild on softwareglimpse.com treats products as entities: research, comparisons, pricing tools, and recommendations that are fit-based. I will not invent employers, years of experience, or lab tests we did not do.

**Instagram**

Choosing software is expensive to reverse.

That’s why SoftwareGlimpse is no longer “article → affiliate link.” Story in bio.

### Sat 22 Aug — Best for this team (B, carousel)

**Facebook**

It’s which CRM fits this team size, this pipeline, this admin appetite, this budget. Our best-CRM page is a shortlist with a methodology — not a trophy.

Use it to narrow, then run the Finder with *your* constraints.

## 8. Reply bank (DMs and comments)

| They say | You send |
| --- | --- |
| “Which CRM should I use?” | “I won’t guess from a comment. Use the CRM Finder — it shortlists from your constraints: {url}. If you tell me team size + inbound vs outbound, I can point you to the right *tool*, not a hot take.” |
| “Is HubSpot worth it?” | “Depends on whether you need the suite. Product page {url} + Plan Selector {url}. I won’t quote a live price here.” |
| “Are you paid by X?” | “We may earn a commission if you buy through some links. Finder order and scores do not use that. {affiliate disclosure url} {editorial independence url}” |
| Vendor complaint | “Happy to correct facts if you point at the page and the source. We won’t change a ranking because of a partnership.” |
| “Do you have a newsletter?” | Only if the provider is actually configured. Otherwise: “Not live yet — grab the CRM templates here: /resources/.” |

Never close a DM with a vendor affiliate URL as the first link. Tool first, review second, commercial CTA last (and only on the site).

## 9. After week 4

Do not write 24 more weeks of captions in advance. Clone the formula that got **Facebook link clicks**. If Reels get views but no clicks, the hook is entertainment — tighten the last 5 seconds to a single URL spoken slowly.

Refresh this file only when a formula dies or a new category finder becomes the thing you actually want to push.
