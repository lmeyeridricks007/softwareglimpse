# Product vision

## Problem

The WordPress SoftwareGlimpse site followed:

`SEO article → review → affiliate link → commission`

That model underperformed. Content was page-centric rather than decision-centric.

## Solution

Rebuild on `softwareglimpse.com` as a structured publishing + decision platform:

```text
Search / AI discovery
  → structured content
  → software discovery
  → comparison
  → calculators / tools
  → personalized recommendations
  → affiliate conversion
```

Affiliate monetization remains, but ranking and recommendations are fit-based.

## North-star

Help a buyer answer “which software should I choose?” with:

- comparable structured facts
- explainable recommendations
- cost estimates
- editorial integrity

## Initial commercial wedge

**CRM + Sales Software** (existing affiliate traction).

Seed products (identity only until researched):

Pipedrive, Freshsales, Close, Salesflare, folk, Keap, Streak, Capsule, Apollo.io, Lusha, RocketReach, Amplemarket, Closely.

Architecture must remain category-agnostic.

## Primary taxonomy

1. CRM & Sales  
2. Sales Intelligence & Lead Generation  
3. Business Communications  
4. Customer Service  
5. Marketing & Growth  
6. Project Management & Productivity  
7. HR, Workforce & Training  
8. AI Software  
9. IT & Development  
10. Ecommerce  

Plus cross-cutting: industries, business sizes, team types, use cases, features, integrations, deployment, pricing models.

## Product principles

| Principle | Implication |
| --- | --- |
| Decision > article | Page types exist to help choose, not only to rank |
| One entity | Never duplicate Pipedrive across categories |
| Integrity | Affiliate availability ≠ ranking |
| Evidence | Facts need provenance when claimed |
| Quality SEO | No auto-generated empty combos |
| AI-ready | Schemas first; generation later |
