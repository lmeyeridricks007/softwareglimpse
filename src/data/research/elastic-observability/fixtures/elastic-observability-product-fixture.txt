SHORT_DESCRIPTION: Elastic Observability is Elastic’s metrics, logs, traces, and APM solution on Elastic Cloud — not the Elasticsearch search product alone. Elastic Cloud Hosted Standard publishes as low as $99/month for a small production config (120 GB storage / 2 zones on elastic.co/pricing/cloud-hosted). Serverless Observability is usage-based ingest (commonly cited ~$0.07–$0.09/GB). Self-managed Elastic Stack remains a separate OSS/licence path. Same observability-monitoring cluster as Datadog and Grafana Cloud.
VENDOR_POSITIONING: Open observability on Elastic Cloud — logs, metrics, and traces without treating search-only Elasticsearch as the SKU.
