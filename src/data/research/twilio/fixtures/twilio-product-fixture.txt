# FIXTURE SNAPSHOT — structured from first-party research (2026-08-17)

SHORT_DESCRIPTION: Twilio is a programmable communications platform (CPaaS): developers embed SMS, voice, WhatsApp, video and related channels via APIs, with optional Twilio Flex as a build-your-own cloud contact center. First-party pricing on twilio.com/en-us/pricing (checked 2026-08-17) is usage-based — US SMS from $0.0083 per message, Voice from ~$0.0085/min inbound and ~$0.014/min outbound, WhatsApp from $0.005 per message (use-case dependent), plus number rents and carrier fees. Flex publishes named-user floors from $150/user/month, active-user-hour from $1, and a User+usage model from $35 MAU (flex pricing page 2026-08-17). Free trial / start-for-free path; no SMB softphone seat card. Scored as a developer platform — landscape adjacent only; not a phone-system peer to RingCentral/OpenPhone.
