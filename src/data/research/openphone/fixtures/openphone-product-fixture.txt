# FIXTURE SNAPSHOT — structured from first-party research (2026-08-17)

SHORT_DESCRIPTION: OpenPhone (rebranded Quo in 2025; product surface unchanged) is a modern shared business phone for SMB teams: one local or toll-free number per user, unlimited US/Canada calling and SMS under fair use, shared inboxes, and Sona AI answering. Starter $15, Business $23 and Scale $35 per user/month annual ($19/$33/$47 monthly) were confirmed on openphone.com/pricing 2026-08-17. Phone menus, automatic recording and deeper CRM (HubSpot/Salesforce) land on Business+; Scale adds AI call tags and dedicated onboarding. Fair-use policy prohibits cold calling and auto-dialers — not a power-dialer product.
VENDOR_POSITIONING: The modern phone system for modern teams — shared business numbers, SMS and AI that feel like a product your team will actually use.
